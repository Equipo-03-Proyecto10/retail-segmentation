Two fills, two foregrounds — not interchangeable. --mq-accent (700) is the interactive fill and the only one that takes --mq-accent-on, white; its hover and active steps go darker in the light theme, to 800 and 900, so the pairing holds at 6.4:1, 9.4:1 and 12.4:1. --mq-accent-bold (500) is the brand papaya used for fills, rails and the wordmark; white on it reads 2.85:1, so it takes --mq-accent-bold-on, black, at 7.4:1. Text on the subtle tint uses --mq-accent-subtle-on (900) at 10.5:1. Ratios above are against white; 600 is a focus-ring colour and carries no text at any size. The dark theme inverts the ladder — the fill lightens on hover and black is the only legal foreground on all of it.

Colour-vision behaviour. This is Paul Tol’s muted qualitative scheme taken verbatim, in its published order, minus wine — the nearest neighbour to both rose and purple. Nothing is mixed, tinted or interpolated, so the separations under deuteranopia, protanopia and tritanopia are the ones Tol verified rather than ones we asserted. Adjacent pairs also differ in lightness, so the set survives a greyscale print. Eight is a ceiling rather than a preference: no published colour-vision-safe qualitative scheme supplies more than eight or nine, and a ninth simultaneous series is a charting decision — split the view, small-multiple it, or collapse the tail into cat-bad. The dark theme swaps to Tol’s light scheme, hue-paired slot for slot, so a series survives a theme switch. Segment badges always render the label code beside the swatch, and colour maps to the stable segment label, never to the run-scoped id.

Density is a class on any ancestor and retargets the whole tier-3 control geometry, not just the table: row and header height, cell padding, input and button heights, card and panel padding, panel header, nav and menu items, tabs, pagination, tree rows, toolbar and top bar, badge, pill and chip heights, and the stack, field and row gaps. Type size holds at 13px through comfortable and compact, so changing density re-times the rhythm without reflowing copy; only dense drops to 12px. Dense controls stand at 24px, below the 44px touch minimum — pointer input only, never on mobile.

Focus ring

outline: 2px solid var(--mq-focus-ring); outline-offset: 1px, applied through :focus-visible on every interactive element — buttons, inputs, selects, checkboxes, radios, toggles, table sort headers, pagination, navigation items, chip removers. The ring is papaya-600 so it stays visible against papaya-700 fills. It is never removed and never replaced by a background change alone.

Radius and elevation

radius-1 2px · radius-2 4px (default) · radius-3 6px (maximum)

Two elevation levels exist: --mq-elevation-popover for menus, tooltips and toasts, and --mq-elevation-modal for the dialog and the drawer. Everything else is a 1px border. No pills, no 12px cards, no third shadow.
