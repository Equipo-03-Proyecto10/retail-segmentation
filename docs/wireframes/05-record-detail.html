Consulta de información
  1. A definition list of facts, no editing controls. The same shape serves the product and segment details. Missing values render as — or "None recorded", never blank or "null".
  2. Cross-links only where a relationship exists and the viewer may follow it: the segment links to the segment detail (which lists its members), closing the customer ↔ segment loop the demonstration shows.
  3. Every detail page has one explicit way back to its list. On a product detail an "Edit" link appears here too — only for catalog.write.

Low-fidelity wireframe. Structure and intent only — the visual design (type, colour, spacing, components) is decided separately in docs/design-system/ and applied in the Jinja2 template. Nothing here is functional.
