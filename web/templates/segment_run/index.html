Segment run

Score every customer's recency, frequency and spend over a window of recorded sales, match the result against the segment rules, and write the segment each customer belongs in.

Scores are quintiles across the customer base, 5 being the most recent, the most frequent and the highest spend. A customer with no sales in the window is left unassigned rather than keeping a stale segment, and a customer whose scores match no rule is left unassigned and counted below.
